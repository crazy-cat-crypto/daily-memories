About Daily Memories

A Computer Science project by Aayush Baral · Class XI · 2026

What is this?

Daily Memories is a personal journaling platform that uses email as the writing interface. Instead of opening yet another app, you simply send an email — and your entry is saved, timestamped, and waiting for you when you log in.

The idea came from the frustration of journaling apps that feel like chores. Email is already something you do. Why not make it count?

How does it work?

When you send an email to daily-memories@baral-aayush.com.np, the server parses the sender, subject, and body — then stores it linked to your account. The subject line becomes your entry title, and the body becomes your journal content.

You can view all entries on your dashboard, sorted chronologically, and edit any entry directly in your browser.

Security & Privacy

Your entries are linked to your account only. Passwords are hashed — never stored in plain text. Sessions are secured with a secret key. The database is hosted on Neon PostgreSQL with SSL-enforced connections.

This is a student project. Your journal is private, but this is not a commercial product with enterprise-level security guarantees.

About the developer

Hi, I'm Aayush Baral — a student from Nepal currently in Class XI (2025–2026), studying Physics, Mathematics, Chemistry, and Computer Science. This project was built as my Computer Science practical work.

The backend is built with Flask and SQLAlchemy, the database is PostgreSQL on Neon, and the app is deployed on Render. The email receiving is handled via a custom pipeline.
